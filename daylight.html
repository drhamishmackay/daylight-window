Time outside if you take every window—

The number that matters. Each window shows how long you could stay before your skin would just start to pink, and the bar shows how much of that allowance the day’s windows use up. Staying well inside it means no burn, and the ultraviolet you do collect is the small, slow kind — which is what adds up over decades into ageing. The aim is the most hours outside for the least of that bar.

How windows are picked. The app reads the hourly forecast for your exact spot, cloud cover already included, and marks every stretch of daylight sitting at or under your hard limit.

Honest caveat. No amount of ultraviolet is completely free of risk — the risk falls smoothly as UV falls but never reaches zero. This finds the gentlest daylight of your day. It is not medical advice, and it assumes sunscreen stays on your face regardless.
